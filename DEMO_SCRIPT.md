# 3–5 Minute Demo Walkthrough

**0:00–0:30 — Introduce the problem**
- Explain that roster planning must respect brand calendars, employee availability, weekly offs, leave, and process coverage.
- State that the dataset is fictional and contains 30 employees across 12 brands and five pods.

**0:30–1:10 — Show the operating model**
- Open Overview and show employees, brands, processes, pods, and calendar rules.
- Open Team & allocations and explain single-resource and multi-resource brand examples.

**1:10–2:10 — Generate a roster**
- Choose the month and year.
- Click Generate / regenerate roster.
- Show assigned records, coverage-gap records, and CSV export.

**2:10–3:00 — Demonstrate a change**
- Open Leave & holidays, add approved leave for an employee assigned to a brand on a date when that brand operates.
- Regenerate the roster.
- Open Validation and show the affected allocation flagged as a coverage gap with a suggested next action.

**3:00–4:00 — Show stakeholder views and validation**
- Filter by pod, brand, process, and employee.
- Explain that mandatory availability and coverage issues are surfaced for manager review.

**4:00–4:30 — Explain trade-offs**
- Mention that the MVP focuses on transparent baseline validation. Shift overlap detection, automatic backup optimization, persistent storage, and approval workflows are future improvements, not completed features.
